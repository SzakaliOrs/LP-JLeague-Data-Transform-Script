SwissTableLeague → SwissStandings Converter

  • Paste a whole {{SwissTableLeague|...}} block into the left box.
  • If the input has matchGroupId1=, matchGroupId2=, ..., those ids and that round count are used as-is (typing a Matchgroup prefix overrides them).
  • Otherwise Total rounds is worked out from the team count and the old rounds= (games per team): 12 teams and rounds=33 → 3 legs → 33 rounds. With an odd team count one team sits out each round, so 13 teams and rounds=24 → 2 legs of 13 → 26 rounds.
  • Otherwise Matchgroup prefix defaults to the title's initials plus the season, e.g. "Bangladesh Football League 2026-27" → BFL2627, giving BFL2627R01, BFL2627R02, ...
  • Round titles use roundtitle= if set (e.g. "Matchweek 1"), otherwise "Round 1".
  • Add teams (one per line) fills empty teamN= slots in order, then adds any extra teams to the end. Slots that stay empty are left out. If no teams are left at all, the output gets |importopponents=true instead.
  • Rounds up to Rounds finished get started=1|finished=1; rounds up to Rounds started get started=1|finished=0.
